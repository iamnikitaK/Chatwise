If this were my competition project, I'd add one feature that most teams won't have:

Multimodal AI

Instead of relying only on glove sensors:

Mode 1: Camera-only sign recognition (works without the glove).

Mode 2: Glove-only recognition (works in poor lighting).

Mode 3: Camera + glove together, with the AI intelligently fusing both inputs for higher accuracy.


This keeps the hardware simple but makes the AI substantially more sophisticated and novel. It demonstrates multimodal learning, robustness, personalization, and real-world usability—exactly the kind of AI contribution that stands out in engineering competitions.
